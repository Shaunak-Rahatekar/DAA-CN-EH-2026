# Ethical Hacking (EH) — Unit I Examination Questions
**Course**: Ethical Hacking (MD***) | **VIT Pune**  
**Unit I**: Introduction to Ethical Hacking & Threat Landscape

---

### Section A: 4-Mark Questions (Short Answer / Conceptual Clarity)
*Estimated time: 7-8 minutes per question*

#### Q1.1 [4 Marks]
Define Vulnerability, Threat, and Exploit. Explain how their interaction leads to Cyber Risk with an illustrative formula and real-world example.

#### Q1.2 [4 Marks]
Differentiate between White Hat, Black Hat, and Grey Hat hackers based on authorization, motivation, and legal standing. Highlight why Grey Hat hacking remains illegal under cyber law despite benevolent intentions.

---

### Section B: 8-Mark Questions (Analytical / Methodological Depth)
*Estimated time: 15-18 minutes per question*

#### Q1.3 [8 Marks]
(a) Contrast Vulnerability Assessment (VA) with Penetration Testing (PT) across 4 technical parameters: objective, methodology, tooling, and business impact. **[4 Marks]**  
(b) Compare Black Box, White Box, and Grey Box penetration testing methodologies in terms of tester perspective, cost, thoroughness, and detection simulation. Provide one ideal enterprise use case for each. **[4 Marks]**

#### Q1.4 [8 Marks]
Explain the 5 phases of the CEH Ethical Hacking Methodology in sequential detail. For each phase, describe:
1. The technical objective.
2. Typical techniques/tools used.
3. The specific distinction between how a malicious black hat vs an authorized ethical hacker acts during that phase. **[8 Marks]**

### Section C: 10-Mark Questions (Comprehensive / Scenario-Based)
*Estimated time: 22-25 minutes*

#### Q1.5 [10 Marks]
(a) Walk through the 7 stages of the **Lockheed Martin Cyber Kill Chain** from an adversary's perspective using a realistic enterprise ransomware attack scenario (e.g., weaponized spear-phishing email targeting an accounting department). For each stage, identify an enterprise defensive control capable of breaking the kill chain. **[6 Marks]**  
(b) Analyze the Indian **Information Technology (IT) Act 2000** as amended in 2008:
1. Explain the legal provisions and penalties under **Section 43, Section 66, Section 66C, Section 66D, and Section 66F**.
2. Outline the 4 mandatory legal prerequisites (e.g., NDA, RoE, Scope, Authorization letter) an ethical hacker must execute before initiating any penetration test on a client network to avoid prosecution under Section 66. **[4 Marks]**
